NOVEMBER 1, 2026 · LAB ANNOUNCEMENT

Sunil GC joins the University of Tennessee, Knoxville.

Dr. Sunil GC has joined the University of Tennessee, Knoxville as an Assistant Professor of Sensing and Data Analytics in the Department of Entomology and Plant Pathology, UT Institute of Agriculture.

With his arrival, he has launched the GC PhytoEnto Sensing & AI Lab. The lab develops sensing and AI tools to study agroecosystems, combining acoustic sensing; RGB, multispectral, hyperspectral, and thermal imaging; and machine learning, deep learning, and computer vision to support crop and pest management decisions from the greenhouse to the field.

Sunil earned his Ph.D. at North Dakota State University and was a postdoctoral researcher with NDSU and USDA-ARS, working on hyperspectral imaging and deep learning for crop stress detection.
