COLLABORATION

Good science
grows together.

We welcome collaborations that bring sensing, imaging, and artificial intelligence to questions across agroecosystems: how plants, insect pests, pathogens, and beneficial organisms interact with each other and their environment. Combining expertise helps us turn those insights into practical tools for growers and researchers.
