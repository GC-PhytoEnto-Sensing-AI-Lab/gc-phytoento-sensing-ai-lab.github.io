GC PhytoEnto Sensing & AI Lab

Intelligent sensing for plant health and insect science, at the Department of Entomology and Plant Pathology, University of Tennessee, Knoxville.

About the lab

Crop diseases and insect pests often do their damage before anyone can see it. Our lab develops sensing and artificial intelligence methods that detect crop stress, plant diseases, and insect pests early, at the pre-symptomatic stage, so growers and researchers can act sooner.

We combine hyperspectral imaging, chemical sensing, and deep learning, and work from controlled greenhouse studies to the field.

Research

  • Pre-symptomatic disease and stress detection

    Using hyperspectral imaging to find spectral signatures of infection and stress, such as freeze injury, before symptoms appear.

  • Insect pest sensing

    Detecting insect pests and their activity through volatile organic compound (VOC) sensing and thermal imaging.

  • AI for spectral data

    Building deep learning models that learn from spectral and spatial information together, and that hold up across environments, seasons, and sensors.

  • From greenhouse to field

    Turning lab methods into practical tools, with automated imaging systems, UAV surveys, and edge devices for real-time detection.
