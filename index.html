ABOUT THE LAB

Better signals.
Earlier answers.

Agroecosystems are shaped by interactions among plants and beneficial and harmful organisms, including insect pests and pathogens. Our lab develops sensing and AI methods to study these organisms and detect meaningful changes in their environment.

From controlled greenhouse studies to the field, we bring together RGB, multispectral, hyperspectral, and thermal imaging, acoustic sensing, and deep learning to better understand agricultural ecosystems.
